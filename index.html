About Us

Visser Construction is owned and operated by Marshall Visser, a licensed contractor serving Northern California. Whether you're planning a remodel, an addition, a repair, or a new build, we bring the same care and attention to every project, no matter the size.

We work directly with every client, from the first conversation to the final walkthrough, so you always know who's responsible for your project. We keep you informed along the way, answer your questions, and treat your home or property with respect while we work.

Our goal on every job is the same: quality work that lasts and a client who's happy with the result.
