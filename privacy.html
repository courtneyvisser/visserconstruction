Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Visser Construction LLC ("Visser Construction," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our website, visserconstruction.net (the "Site"). We keep this Site simple and collect as little information as possible.

Information We Collect

The Site does not have contact forms, account sign-ups, or newsletter subscriptions, and it does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not collect personal information through the Site itself.

If you choose to contact us by phone or email, we receive the information you share with us, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your project address and details about your project
  • Any other information you choose to include

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us only to:

  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Discuss, estimate, and complete your project
  • Keep records related to our work, as required by law

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only when needed to respond to you or complete your project, or when required by law.

Website Hosting and Email

Like most websites, the Site relies on a few service providers to operate:

  • Hosting: The Site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically log basic technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit, for security and to keep the Site running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Domain and email: Our domain is managed through Cloudflare, and emails sent to our @visserconstruction.net addresses are forwarded through Cloudflare's email routing service to our inboxes. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The Site's fonts and images are hosted on the Site itself, so your browser does not contact any other third-party services when you view our pages.

Do Not Track Signals

Some web browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting. Because the Site does not track visitors or allow third parties to track your activity over time or across other websites, the Site works the same whether or not this setting is turned on.

Links to Other Websites

The Site includes links to other websites, such as the Contractors State License Board (cslb.ca.gov). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Reviewing or Changing Your Information

If you have shared information with us and would like to review, correct, or delete it, please contact us using the information below. We will do our best to help, subject to any records we are required to keep by law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date at the top.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact courtney@visserconstruction.net.
